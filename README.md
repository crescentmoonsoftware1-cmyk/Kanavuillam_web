# Kanavu Illam Web (கனவு இல்லம்)

Official web portal and architectural plan analysis showcase for **Kanavu Illam** — transforming 2D residential floor plans and blueprints into millimeter-accurate 3D BIM cutaways, 8-zone Vāstu audits, itemized PWD cost BOQs, and code-verified structural schedules.

---

## 🚀 Features

* **3D BIM Spatial Reconstruction:** Millimeter-accurate floor plan reconstruction with interactive LOD 400 cutaway walkthroughs.
* **8-Zone Vāstu Audit:** Traditional Vedic orientation scoring across all 8 cardinal quadrants with Ishanya entrance alignment.
* **Tamil Nadu PWD Cost BOQ:** Live schedule rate takeoff with itemized bills of quantities for cement, steel, masonry, and labor.
* **IS 456 & IS 1893 Structural Schedules:** Seismic Zone III certified frame stress, rebar cut schedules, and footing calculations.
* **Mobile App Download:** Direct download link for the official Android release build (`assets/KanavuIllam.apk`).
* **Compliance & Legal:** Fully integrated Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy approved for Razorpay payment gateway verification.
