DPDP Act & Google Play Verified

Request Account Deletion

Under Google Play Developer Policies and Section 8 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the absolute right to permanently delete your account and personal data at any time.

What is permanently purged: Your personal profile, login credentials, saved architectural blueprints, 3D parametric CAD models, Vāstu compliance scores, and analysis history are permanently removed. Financial invoice records are retained only for the statutory period required by Indian tax and GST accounting laws.
Deletion Request Submitted

In accordance with Section 8 of the DPDP Act 2023, your account and associated architectural assets will be permanently purged within statutory timelines. An email notification has been dispatched.

1. Statutory Deletion Timelines

Upon submission of this verified request, our automated retention scrubber initiates the immediate revocation of your authentication credentials, cloud sessions, and access tokens. All stored Floor Plans, CAD vectors, 3D renderings, and generated Vāstu dossiers will be erased from active databases within 7 calendar days.

2. Financial Record Retention Exception

As mandated by Section 128 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Section 36 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (CGST Act), invoices, GST billing records, and transaction logs relating to paid architectural consultations or services must be retained for the mandatory statutory period of eight (8) financial years. These records are strictly archived in read-only audit logs and cannot be used for any marketing or profiling activities.
